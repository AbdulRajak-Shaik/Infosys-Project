# AgroAI User Manual

This manual explains how farmers can use the AgroAI platform to analyze soil, recommendations, and manage crop health.

---

## 1. Registering & Selecting Language
1. Open the application in your browser.
2. Click **Get Started** and fill in your registration details (Name, Email, password, and region).
3. Select your preferred language (English, Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Santali, etc.) from the header drop-down menu. All cards, fields, and predictions are dynamically translated in real-time.

---

## 2. Soil Analysis & Crop Suitability
1. Navigate to **AI Modules** -> **Soil Classification**.
2. Click **Upload File** or **Use Camera** to upload a soil sample image.
3. If the uploaded image is too dark, blurry, low resolution, or solid color, the system will reject it with a helpful validation message.
4. Input your field parameters: **Nitrogen (N)**, **Phosphorus (P)**, **Potassium (K)**, and **Soil pH**.
5. Locate and fetch your weather data by clicking **Detect GPS** or searching for your district.
6. Click **Predict Best Crop**.
7. View the classified soil type, the suitability score of candidate crops, and the recommended fertilizer dosage schedule.
8. Click **Download PDF Report** to save or print the agronomic plan.

---

## 3. Submitting Prediction Feedback
* Scroll to the bottom of the result card.
* Under "Help improve our models", select **👍 Prediction Correct** or **👎 Prediction Incorrect**.
* Write any optional comments in the text box and click **Submit Feedback**.
