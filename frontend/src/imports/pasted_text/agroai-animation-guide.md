Core Objective:
I want to transform AgroAI into a premium AI-powered agricultural platform by adding smooth, meaningful, and modern animations throughout the application.

Strict Design & Style Constraints:

UI Constraints: Do not redesign the UI. Keep the current layouts, typography, navigation, spacing, and branding exactly the same.

Animation Style: All custom animations must follow a minimalist, continuous line-drawing/vector stroke style (fluid, simple outline animations without heavy fills).

Color Palette: The line-art strokes and particle effects must strictly match AgroAI’s exact brand color design scheme.

Motion Feel: Every animation should feel elegant, lightweight, premium, and purposeful. Avoid flashy effects, heavy 3D, or excessive motion.

Inspiration: The motion language should be inspired by modern applications like Apple, Linear, Stripe, Notion, Arc Browser, ChatGPT, and Framer, while uniquely utilizing farming and AI concepts.

Please replace generic loading indicators, empty states, and transitions with these specific, agriculture-inspired line-drawing animations:

1. Application Launch Animation
When the application opens, do not instantly display the dashboard. Show a branded splash animation lasting approximately 2–3 seconds.
Sequence:

Background fades in using AgroAI's theme color.

A smooth, continuous outline draws a seed dropping into the soil.

The line branches downward to form small roots.

The line extends upward, drawing a tiny sprout.

The sprout fluidly develops into a healthy plant with leaves.

Subtle AI circuit lines briefly animate through the outline of the stem and leaves.

The AgroAI logo text fades in below the plant.

Directly beneath the logo, display the quote: "Growing Intelligence for Agriculture"

Transition smoothly into the dashboard.

2. Network Connectivity Animation
Instead of displaying generic errors, show an engaging line-art animation.

Offline: A continuous line draws a small plant trying to receive water. Rain clouds disappear, and the plant gently droops. A Wi-Fi icon blinks softly in the brand color. Message: "Waiting for a stronger connection..."

Reconnected: Rain begins (drawn as dashed lines). The plant slowly animates back to a healthy state. The Wi-Fi icon turns solid green. Automatically continue without page refresh.

3. AI Crop Prediction Animation
Replace the current rotating loading spinner. Total duration approx 4–6 seconds. Avoid percentage counters.
Sequence:

Step 1: The uploaded soil image appears.

Step 2: A futuristic AI scanner line sweeps across the image.

Step 3: Minimalist stroke particles emerge from the soil.

Step 4: Vector outline icons representing Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, Soil pH, and Weather orbit around a central plant outline.

Step 5: These parameters flow into a glowing AI core.

Step 6: The AI core transforms into a growing crop using a continuous drawing effect.

Step 7: The crop blooms.

Step 8: Display text sequentially: "Analyzing Soil Texture" ↓ "Matching Climate" ↓ "Comparing 22 Crop Profiles" ↓ "Generating AI Recommendation" ↓ "Finalizing Prediction".

4. Soil Image Upload Animation

File gently drops into the upload area.

Upload border glows with the brand's green.

AI scanner line scans the image.

Image smoothly transitions into a preview.

Display: "Soil image successfully analyzed."

5. Weather Retrieval Animation
Instead of a spinner, display a smooth morphing line-art sequence:

Clouds ↓ Sunlight ↓ Rain droplets ↓ Wind particles ↓ Temperature icon ↓ Humidity icon ↓ Rainfall icon.

These merge into a weather card.

Message: "Fetching live weather for your location..."

6. Dashboard Loading Skeleton
Every dashboard should initially show animated skeleton placeholders. Cards should shimmer gently, charts should animate in, statistics should count upward, and graphs should draw themselves using fluid vector lines.

7. AI Chatbot Thinking Animation
Replace three bouncing dots.

Show a tiny line-drawn plant inside a circular AI ring.

Leaves gently move, and AI outline particles rotate.

The ring pulses while the assistant thinks.

When the response is ready: The plant blossoms slightly. The message appears with a smooth typing effect.

8. Disease Detection Animation

AI scanner line passes over the uploaded leaf.

Healthy portions glow green; diseased regions glow amber or red.

The detected disease outline icon appears.

Confidence percentage animates upward.

Recommendation card slides into view.

9. Soil Classification Animation

Vector soil particles separate, colors blend.

The AI identifies texture.

The detected soil type grows from the ground like a label emerging from the soil.

Display confidence using a smooth circular radial animation.

10. Weather Dashboard Animation
Weather outline icons should be subtly animated:

Sunny: Sun rays rotate gently.

Rain: Dashed line raindrops fall continuously.

Wind: Vector leaves move.

Clouds: Slow drifting animation.

Storm: Lightning flashes subtly.

Night: Moon glows gently.

11. Notification Animation

New notification: Bell icon gently shakes. Small green pulse expands. Badge smoothly scales into view.

Opening notifications: Cards fade upward.

Marking as read: Card gently fades. Unread badge decreases with animation.

12. Success Animation
Instead of a basic toast: A continuous line draws a seed growing into a small plant. A green checkmark elegantly draws itself within the leaves. Toast disappears gracefully.

13. Error Animation
Instead of red error boxes: Show a small, wilted line-art plant with drooping leaves. Helpful guidance appears. When corrected, the plant outline animates to recover and become green again.

14. Empty States
Replace plain illustrations with simple, elegant animated line-art:

No Crop History: Empty field with a small sprout drawing itself.

No Notifications: Mailbox shaped like a tree with resting birds.

No Soil Analysis: Fresh soil waiting for the first sample.

No Weather Data: Clouds waiting to gather.

15. Page Transition Animation
Every transition should feel natural (200–300 milliseconds).

Current page: Fade + slight scale down.

Next page: Fade + slide upward.

16. Card Hover Effects
Desktop: Cards elevate slightly, soft shadow appears, tiny leaf particles appear briefly, and the border glows green.

17. Button Animations

Hover: Slight lift, gradient brightens.

Pressed: Compress naturally.

Success: Small outline ripple resembling a water droplet.

18. Progress Indicators
Instead of plain progress bars, use growing line-art vines or stems. As progress increases, leaves are drawn. Flowers bloom at 100%.

19. Authentication Animation

Login: A padlock outline transforms seamlessly into a leaf outline. Leaf unfolds. Dashboard loads.

Signup: Seed is planted, roots outline themselves, account successfully created.

20. Logout Animation
Plant gently waves, leaves float away, screen fades, return to landing page.

21. 404 Page
Instead of plain text: A smooth line-art animation of a farmer searching for a crop in a field. Signboard reads: "Looks like this page hasn't been planted yet." Button: "Return Home".

22. Server Error Animation
A line-art robot farmer repairing an irrigation pipe. Message: "Our AI is working in the field. Please try again shortly."

23. Mobile Gestures

Pull to refresh: A seed line-drawing grows while pulling. On release, the plant blooms.

Swipe actions: Smooth spring animations.

Bottom navigation: Outline icons gently bounce and fill in slightly when selected.

24. Accessibility
Respect the user's Reduce Motion system preference. When enabled: Remove decorative animations, keep only essential transitions, and ensure animations never interfere with usability.
